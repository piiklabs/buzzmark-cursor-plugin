# Buzzmark for Cursor

Buzzmark is a ghostwriter that writes, plans and schedules social media posts for founders and growing businesses, in their own voice. This plugin connects Cursor to your Buzzmark account, so you can ask for post ideas and drafts, or plan your week, without leaving the editor.

## What you can ask

- "Write 3 posts for X about what we shipped this week, in my brand's voice."
- "Check this post against my brand voice, then save it as a draft."
- "Plan next week's posts around my best posting times and schedule them."
- "What worked on LinkedIn this month?"

Posts can go to Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, X, Threads and TikTok, each with its own version for that platform.

## Before you start

You need a Buzzmark account with a plan and at least one social account connected in Buzzmark. New accounts get 7 days free at [buzzmark.ai](https://buzzmark.ai).

## Connect

1. Install Buzzmark from the Cursor Marketplace.
2. The first time Cursor uses it, Buzzmark opens a sign-in page. Sign in and allow Cursor. There is no API key to copy.
3. Ask Cursor: "What social accounts do I have in Buzzmark?"

To connect without the plugin, the [setup guide](https://buzzmark.ai/ai-apps/cursor) has the server URL and config.

## What Cursor can do in your account

Buzzmark never posts on the spot. Cursor can save drafts and schedule, move or unschedule posts, and every scheduled post stays on your Buzzmark calendar, where you can change or remove it before it goes out. Cursor works only with the brands, accounts and posts in the Buzzmark account you sign in to.
